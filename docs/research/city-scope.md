# Harbor City: geography and physical-place requirements

This catalogue expands the full [LOWLIGHT brief](../brief.md) into named city
areas, transport topology and physical places. The companion
[city-source-map.json](city-source-map.json) contains **425 planned records** and
**84 reference entries**. Its `research_complete` flag remains `false`: the neighbourhood
identity baseline and transit service sequences are enumerated, while individual
interior/access/placement and broader category audits remain open. Nothing in
this document certifies a produced or playable city.

## Source baseline and counting

Adding **nine visible Alderney towns and industrial areas** gives a **65-area
baseline** across the playable metropolitan world. The three minor islands are
included in that baseline. A prison is retained as a separate facility rather
than silently being counted as another neighbourhood. [Liberty City index](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Liberty_City_%28HD_Universe%29),
[Alderney areas](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Alderney).

## Original geography and identity

Harbor City receives original names, shapes, street layouts, architecture,
signage, institutions and environmental fiction. Source map geometry and
protected building/art designs are not project assets. The source teaches the
value of layered public space, waterfronts, distinct neighbourhoods, ordinary
services and meaningful traversal; the new city authors those roles in its own
setting.

The proposed subareas are deliberately varied:

- **Breakwater:** Crane Ward, Saltgate, Kiln Row, Dispatch Quarter, Pier Eight,
  Lantern Boardwalk, Breakwater Courts, Old Quay, Orchard Rise, Signal Hill,
  Founders Walk and Rainstep. Warehouses/underpasses, villas, markets, coastal
  entertainment, housing courts, park slopes and clinics have distinct paths and
  spatial purposes.
- **Saint Brigid:** Brigid Market, Pump Island, Foundry Reach, Harbor Airfield,
  Cedar Terrace, Worlds Garden, Bellhaven and Flightpath. A multi-level station,
  utility island, arts workshops, airport logistics, suburban streets, exhibition
  park and pub/cemetery coast provide different reasons to travel.
- **The Narrows:** Northwater, Canal Works, Foundry Steps, Dry Basin, Tidal Corner,
  Longcourt and Canal Refuge. Parks, scrapyards, steep retail streets, unfinished
  works, coastal housing, large estates and a refuge home form distinct routes
  and public/private spaces.
- **Glassward:** East Assembly, Guildbank, Civic Orchard, Orchard East, Orchard
  West, West Assembly, Reservoir Ward, Scholar Heights, Beacon Island, Grand
  Union, Spire Gardens, Ledger East, Coldstore, Civic Reach, Lowlight Row, Neon
  Cross, Three Corners, River Quarter, Seawall Quarter, South Lantern, Lantern
  Market, Civic Square, Bourse, Auction Quay, Southbank, Lantern Island, Copper
  Table, Lower Ledger and Arts Quarter. The core needs actual variation in
  skyline, frontage, parks, campuses, nightlife, institutional space, housing,
  piers and service alleys.
- **Ironhaven:** Cliffside, North Foundry, Ironhaven Centre, Brickhaven, Forge
  Ward, Anchor Reach, Furnace Terrace, Freightgate and Reactor Basin. Hillside
  estates, hospitals, civic towers, close terraces, community/contractor places,
  shipyards, housing beside industry, cargo docks and utility infrastructure have
  separate terrain, access and encounter affordances.

Each neighbourhood row contains an original architectural proposal and at least
two concrete spatial affordances. Those proposals still need authored geometry,
inhabitants, sound and content integration. Names or coloured regions cannot
satisfy the 65-area requirement. A repeated block template also cannot certify a
distinct warehouse undercroft, hillside stair route, park or operating airfield.

## Passenger rail topology

The source uses **26 operating station complexes**, **eight service labels** and
**four named line sections**, combined into **four directional through-services**
on two bidirectional network circuits. Eight service labels do not mean eight
unrelated physical loops. The complete station sequences were recovered from
accessible route references and corroborated against an inspected LTA diagram.
[Service overview](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Subway_in_GTA_IV),
[accessible overview](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Subway_in_GTA_IV),
[LTA diagram provenance](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/File:LibertyCitySubway-GTAIV-map.jpg).

| Source service pair | Direction/continuation role | Proposed original service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A + 8 | Outer-core clockwise service through the port/airport loop | G1 Seaward Loop |
| J + 3 | Reverse outer-core/port/airport service | G2 Landward Loop |
| K + E | Clockwise civic-core service through the canal district | C1 Civic Canal Loop |
| C + B | Reverse civic-core/canal service | C2 Canal Civic Loop |

Ordered source calls, boundary handoffs and corresponding station IDs are in the
JSON; final Harbor City track geometry, distances and station architecture remain
original production work. The source sequences and directions come from
[A + 8](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Route_A),
[J + 3](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Route_J),
[K + E](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Route_K) and
[C + B](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Route_C). The C/B page's introductory
clockwise wording conflicts with its detailed sequence and the overview; the
corroborated reverse sequence is retained and the discrepancy is recorded.

Two distinctions prevent incorrect implementation:

1. The source terminal landmark hosts the Easton subway interchange; it is not
   a second operating subway station. West Park is an operating stop omitted
   from the earlier systems list. The new city map records the corrected station
   role while preserving the existing systems rows/statuses for later explicit
   reconciliation. [Terminal distinction](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Easton_Terminal),
   [West Park](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/West_Park).
2. Huntington Street is a dual-level station complex visited twice on a complete
   port/airport circuit. Its upper and lower calls are distinct. The new graph
   preserves both visits instead of connecting crossed tracks at ground level.
   Easton remains a shared interchange between the two network circuits.
   [Huntington levels](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Huntington_Street_Station),
   [port line](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Broker_Line).

There is also a closed/renovated station, a service yard, abandoned rail sections
and a maintenance junction. These are authored world spaces with access and
routing policies. A closed branch is not an extra scheduled passenger route.
Exact street portals, platform geometry and the full entrance inventory remain a
research gate. Source trains also have progression/pursuit restrictions that the
original service rules must account for; a moving prop alone does not implement
passenger transit. [Rail access and restrictions](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Subway_in_GTA_IV).

The gondola is a separate physical service with mainland/island stations, moving
cabins, fare, dwell/doors, boarding, restrictions and arrival. It complements the
island's road bridge and water approaches. The original terminals are Ledger East
Skyline Terminal and Beacon Skyline Terminal. [Gondola role](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Skycar).

## Crossings, shore and infrastructure

The map enumerates 12 crossing records, including three separate spans of the
utility-island interchange, the independently raised rail crossing, a derelict
crossing and an unfinished flyover. Source bridge-family counts do not equal
physical-span counts or the fourteen achievement clearance volumes. The older
Acter/Tudor names describe the same abandoned crossing and remain aliases rather
than two invented bridges. [Bridge families](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Bridges_in_GTA_IV),
[three-span interchange](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/East_Borough_Bridge),
[road/rail distinction](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Northwood_Heights_Bridge),
[derelict crossing](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Acter_Bridge).

Harbor City needs two different main waterways, a broad industrial/recreational
sound, a constrained industrial canal and open coastal water. Its ports, island
boatyard, waterworks, coastal gardens, ship, piers, ferry works and helicopter
sites remain separate place requirements. Every required boat/swim/shore
transition must be physically valid. The source ferry terminals are not proof of
an operating ferry system. [Waterway](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Humboldt_River),
[bay](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Dukes_Bay),
[industrial island facilities](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Charge_Island),
[ferry/industrial district](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Alderney).

Nine road/tunnel records preserve six main road roles and three named tunnel
roles. A long elevated industrial ring must include direction-dependent ramps,
local alternatives and undercroft access. Exact minor-tunnel extent and the
complete street/ramp topology remain open. Three physical toll facilities cover
the interchange, bay crossing and airfield long-stay parking, with real payment,
barrier, queue and public-response behaviour. [Elevated ring](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Plumbers_Skyway),
[coastal-road/tunnel relationship](https://gta.fandom.com/wiki/Union_Drive_West),
[toll locations](https://www.grandtheftwiki.com/Tollbooths).

## Production and remaining research

Each retained original neighbourhood/site needs authored geometry, approaches,
height/shore transitions, appearance, animation/audio context and relevant actor
behaviour. Operating venues need their actual activities or transactions. Mission
interiors need connected rooms, actor paths, usable cover, gates and failure/retry
behaviour. Transit needs correct service topology and physically separate levels.
Source labels on a map, rectangles or generic proximity triggers do not meet the
acceptance profiles.

Twelve explicit research gaps retain the outstanding scope: the broader named
facade/landmark categories; every interior instance and access state; station
portals; minor tunnels and street/ramp extent; all business branches and social
eligibility; vendors/vending/finance/busker/pickup placements; every collectible,
stunt, flight-volume and tour location; every campaign/side-content scene;
episodic/cut contamination; exact borders; original world asset production; and
minor/ambient/media character coverage. These must be resolved before an
exhaustive city research or completed-city claim.

The existing systems gaps and record statuses remain unchanged. Recovering route
names and ordered topology advances the metro research, but exact entrance
layouts and the older station-record reconciliation still remain. The miniature-course objects and named amusement fixtures remain separate
requirements: seven miniature-object roles and 29 named attraction/stall
identities are enumerated, with operational-status audits still required.
